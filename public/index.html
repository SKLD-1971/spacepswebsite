How To Play

PC / Windows Guide

  • 1 Search "Notepad", right-click it, and "Run as administrator".
  • 2 Go to `File > Open` and navigate to `C:\Windows\System32\drivers\etc`.
  • 3 Change file type to "All Files" and open the `hosts` file.
  • 4 Add the server info to the bottom and save the file.

Android Guide

  • 1 Download PowerTunnel from the Play Store.
  • 2 Click the "PowerTunnel URL" button to copy the link.
  • 3 In PowerTunnel, go to `Menu > Host Settings`.
  • 4 Paste the URL and set update period to "On start".

iOS Guide (Surge 5)

  • 1 Download Surge 5 from the App Store.
  • 2 Click the "iOS Surge 5 Config" button to copy the text.
  • 3 In Surge 5, tap "Default", and choose "Edit in Text Mode".
  • 4 Delete all text, paste the config you copied, and save.
